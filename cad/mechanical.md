# LUMA mechanical assembly, revision B

This is an original four-axis prototype design. The complete editable geometry is `cad/luma.scad`; `cad/build.py` exports the printable parts and their exact assembly transforms. Open `cad/assembly.scad` to view the entire assembled STL model with the standard head, or `cad/assembly_dsi.scad` for the optional 4-inch DSI head; `cad/assembly.json` and `cad/assembly_dsi.json` hold their transforms. The master OpenSCAD default view is only a base subassembly preview. For an individual part set `part` to its manifest ID. The illustration meshes in `cad/visual_only` are purchased-component envelopes, not printable replacements.

Revision B changes: the two arm links are enclosed tapered clamshells that hide the servo, bolts and harness (the revision A rails, cross spacers and servo cassettes are gone); the fork plate and both head shells have a 26 × 8 mm centre cable slot; and an optional head accepts the Waveshare 4inch DSI LCD (C). Joint positions, pivot pitches, horn interfaces, the base and the software are unchanged.

The indexed neutral position has a 115-degree upper arm and a 45-degree forearm measured counterclockwise from the forward horizontal (+Y), with the head facing +Y. Both pitch axes run along X. The base yaw axis is Z. The elbow relative angle is -70 degrees. Dimensions: base diameter216mm, upper pivot pitch140mm, forearm pivot pitch120mm, shoulder axis115mm above the base floor datum, head diameter176mm and42mm optical depth. The center of the head's rear mounting plane is approximately Y25.7+21=46.7mm, Z326.7mm; this is not the center of mass. The housing top is414.7mm. Rubber feet add5mm to tabletop height. The 1.85-inch LCD active face is recessed approximately10.8mm behind the diffuser's front surface; the optional 4-inch glass sits6.5mm behind it.

## Vendor geometry and fit datums

The Waveshare ST3215 vendor drawing gives a45.22 x24.72mm side envelope,35mm body axis width,37.25mm across the two horns,19.2mm metal horn diameter and14mm diameter four-hole pattern. The shaft axis is10.11mm from the nearest body end. Files downloaded from Waveshare are in `cad/references/ST3215-2D` and `cad/references/ST3215-3D`. Source: https://www.waveshare.net/wiki/ST3215_Servo and https://www.waveshare.net/w/upload/0/08/ST3215-2D.zip . The supplied drawing does not specify the horn thread; the BOM's M3 horn screws are a purchasing assumption that must be checked on one actual servo before ordering the full set. Never force a mismatched screw. Vendor guidance explicitly warns that a protruding horn screw can contact the servo housing and overload the motor.

Each enclosed link grips the rear30mm of its servo case between the two printed halves, avoiding assumptions about threaded case holes. Both original metal horns remain installed. The far-side factory horn/idler supports the opposite link plate; the design does not place the arm on one unsupported output screw. One purchased6808-2RS bearing supports the yaw platform independently of the motor shaft. The52.15mm bearing pocket and39.75mm neck are intentionally clearance fits. Tune the fit coupon for the actual printer before printing the load-bearing parts.

The standard display is the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.85 with a55 x55mm board/body outline. It is retained at its edges in a55.8mm square pocket. No guessed PCB mounting holes are used. The design allows a12mm board/body stack and uses foam edge shims to take up sample variation. Inspect the purchased screen's actual glass projection and connector position before tightening; the assembly illustration assumes a31.2mm active-face datum. The USB relief is19mm wide; use a low-profile right-angle plug and check its actual bend clearance in the open head before closing it.

The optional display is the Waveshare 4inch DSI LCD (C): a round720 x720 IPS panel in a Ø126mm case,6mm thick, with four M4 tapped bosses Ø7.1 x4mm on a75 x75mm square on its rear, an85 x65mm controller PCB offset toward the connector edge, and components reaching8.2mm behind the boss ends. These figures were measured from the vendor drawing `cad/references/4inch-DSI-LCD-C-details-size.jpg` and the vendor STEP archive `cad/references/4inch_DSI_LCD_C_3D.zip` (Waveshare material, kept for inspection only). The DSI FPC connector position is taken from the vendor photograph and must be confirmed on the purchased unit before the carrier window is trusted.

## Print settings

Print all units in millimetres at100% scale. Largest XY footprint is216 x216mm. On a220mm bed this leaves only2mm per edge: disable an external brim on the base, or use a larger bed if bed clips reduce usable area. Structural parts use PETG,0.20mm layers,4 walls generally,6 walls and40% infill on the four arm link halves. Use solid local infill around the horn and block screws. Print every link half with its5mm plate on the bed: the walls, the through-bolt boss and the servo block then print as vertical features without support; the rear connector notch and the wall cable port are short bridges. Print the head fork with its broad mounting plate against the bed, rotated180 degrees about X from its STL coordinates; enable supports beneath its fork ears. Print the brow bracket with its base plate on the bed (the gussets are45-degree overhangs). Remove support material from all mating surfaces.

Print the two optical diffusers in natural/translucent PETG at0.15mm layers,3 perimeter walls and100% infill. Their optical face is1.2mm thick: specify at least8 solid layers at0.15mm. Smooth the face with600–1000grit paper for frosting, wash and dry it, and test its diffusion with the real rings. The outer diffuser is open behind the LED annulus; it is not a sealed hollow torus. Do not apply frosting, paint or a diffuser over any time-of-flight aperture.

Clear through-holes with the matching drill by hand. The lightly loaded cover bosses have2.8mm plastic-thread-forming pilots for M3 screws; these are not standard metal tapping drills. The2.1mm Pi standoff pilots are tapped M2.5. For frequently serviced covers, enlarge the bosses as necessary in the parametric model and use suitable inserts. Do not use the motor to force a tight printed joint. General designed sliding clearance is0.3–0.4mm. Check flatness and remove elephant's-foot at the bearing, horn, servo pocket and LCD fits. Filament quantity and print duration should be taken from the actual slicer, not the solid CAD volume.

## Prepare purchased metal parts

1. Cut a mild-steel disc180mm diameter and6mm thick, approximately1.19kg before drilling. `cad/ballast_plate.dxf` provides the cut/drill geometry; `cad/ballast_plate.svg` is a dimensioned drill template. With disc center as XY origin, drill four4.4mm holes at(±45,±35) and four3.4mm holes at(±26,-36) and(±26,-8). Deburr and protect the surface from corrosion. The same eight holes exist in the base floor; orient the disc so the asymmetric yaw hole pattern points rearward (-Y). There is no central hole in this version: the passive yaw horn has approximately1.9mm clearance above the steel at the indexed assembly height.
2. Confirm the bearing is a6808 measuring40 x52 x7mm, not a608 skateboard bearing. Check it on a fit coupon or sacrificial test print. Keep the bearing clean; do not hammer it into plastic.
3. Check the servo's original metal horns against the14mm pattern. For the three pitch joints,5mm printed plate +3.3mm printed spacer leaves approximately1.7mm engagement with a10mm screw. For yaw, use a nominal6mm screw through the3mm web plus1mm washer, leaving2mm engagement. Adjust length and washers after checking the real thread depth. These are not torque specifications.

## Assemble the weighted base

4. Put the steel disc on the3mm-thick base floor. Install the yaw cradle on the disc at Z9, aligned with the four asymmetric M3 holes. Use fourM3x16 bolts with washers and nuts below the enclosure. Confirm screw tips and nuts remain above the eventual rubber-foot contact plane.
5. Place servo ID1 in the yaw cradle with its output axis vertical and body extending toward-Y. Its body envelope occupies Z12–47; the output-horn face is approximately Z48.1. Fit the2.6mm keeper above the rear of the case at Z47 and use twoM3x45 bolts through the cradle ears. Tighten evenly only until the case is securely held. The keeper ends below Z50 and clears the lid.
6. Install the electronics tray at Z14 on four5mm metal spacers seated on the steel disc. Use fourM4x25 bolts through base, steel, spacer and tray, with load-spreading washers and lock nuts. The tray and base share the90 x70mm bolt pattern. The large central opening clears the lower yaw case and cradle; four7mm access openings clear the yaw mounting screw heads. Mount the Pi4 on the four integral standoffs with M2.5x6 screws. Mount the small boards using the universal slots, insulated standoffs and cable ties. Keep the external AC adapter and any high-current distribution/fuse enclosure outside this compact base. Put the optional USB speaker outside the base if its84 x43 x32mm case does not fit the chosen cable layout.
7. Route the harness through the13mm rear cable port with a fitted grommet and strain relief. The rectangular secondary port accepts USB/control wiring. Fit four5mm-high rubber feet near radius90mm. Leave a service loop for ±25-degree yaw; this assembly has no slip ring and must not rotate continuously.
8. Seat the6808 bearing in the lid's52.15mm pocket. Place the turntable neck through its40mm bore. Attach the internal3mm horn web to the yaw metal horn using four nominalM3x6 screws and1mm washers. A long hex key reaches the screws through the platform's22mm center aperture. Neck start is Z47.8 and the platform top remains Z68. Check free movement through the limited yaw travel before fastening the lid with sixM3x12 screws.

## Assemble shoulder and enclosed links

Each link is two mirror-image printed halves. Local link coordinates: X runs from the proximal pivot (X0) to the distal servo shaft (X140 upper, X120 forearm); Y is the link height; Z is across the link, with the two5mm plates at Z±22..27 and the2.4mm walls of each half meeting the other half at Z0. The walls begin at X28, so the proximal30mm around the pivot is an open clevis through which the previous joint's servo body and block rotate. The distal24.5mm of each half is the servo block: a pocket floor4.2mm above the plate, pocket walls25.4mm apart, a rear connector notch, and two bolt ears at Y±18.5. One21 x9mm rounded cable port is centred at X87 (upper) or X67 (forearm) on the -Y wall, split equally between the halves; the -Y wall is the inside of the elbow and of the wrist at neutral. A Ø8 half-boss at X35 carries the third through bolt.

9. Bolt the shoulder tower to the turntable using fourM3x16 bolts on the52 x36mm pattern. Install servo ID2 vertically with its shaft axis at Z115 and its case extending downward. Fit the separate keeper at the open side using twoM3x50 bolts. The original two metal horns remain accessible above the clamp.
10. Lay `upper_arm_left` plate-down. Seat servo ID3 in its block pocket with the shaft at the X140 end and the rear connector facing the notch; the front10mm of the case and both horns project beyond the block. Route the elbow servo lead and the forearm/head harness out of the cable port before closing. Place `upper_arm_right` over it so the walls meet at the mid-plane, then fit threeM3x60 bolts with nuts: X35 through the half-bosses and (X115, Y±18.5) through the block ears. The halves hold the rear30mm of the case in a35.6mm pocket; tighten only until the walls touch, never to crush the case. Keep both servo connectors reachable through the notch and preserve the factory horn retaining screws.
11. With ID2 electronically centered at2048, index the upper link at115 degrees above the forward horizontal. Put a3.3mm horn spacer between each metal horn and its printed plate. Secure both sides with four nominalM3x10 screws per horn, eight total, through the proximal horn pattern of each half. Verify engagement depth manually. The passive-side connection must turn without binding, and the shoulder tower keeper must clear the open clevis through the intended travel.
12. Assemble the forearm in the same way from `forearm_left` and `forearm_right` with servo ID4 in the X120 block, threeM3x60 bolts at X35 and (X95, Y±18.5), and the harness leaving through the port on the -Y wall. Index the forearm at45 degrees above the forward horizontal while ID3 is centered; its angle relative to the upper arm is-70 degrees. Attach the proximal plates to both ID3 horns with spacers and eight nominalM3x10 screws.
13. Fit the head fork to both ID4 horns with the remaining two3.3mm spacers and eight nominalM3x10 screws. At indexed neutral, the fork's broad mounting plate faces forward (+Y) and lies21mm in front of the wrist axis. Leave the head off until the unloaded link motion check succeeds. Pass the head harness through the fork plate's26 x8mm slot, into the wrist clevis, over the top of the wrist servo case and into the forearm port; at the elbow it leaves the forearm's open proximal clevis, passes over the elbow servo case and enters the upper link port; at the shoulder it leaves the upper link's open clevis and drops down the tower and through the turntable aperture. Leave a loose loop at each joint that stays slack through the full commissioned travel; the optional printed saddles or cable ties may be used inside the links, never clamping a servo connector or a moving horn.

## Assemble the optical head (standard 1.85-inch display)

All following Z values use the head's rear face as0, increasing toward the viewer; they are not global heights. The head floor occupies0–3, and the shell rim reaches36. The LCD is behind the white ring, allowing its square corners to occupy space that would otherwise intersect the LED annulus. The shell floor and the fork plate share a26 x8mm centre slot for the head harness.

14. Attach the rear shell to the fork with fourM3x12 bolts on its54 x30mm pattern, washers and nuts inside the shell. Install the front, rear, left, right and downward sensor pods using twoM3x10 bolts per pod. The front pod is above the display. The rear pod faces away from it. The side pods' long dimension follows the rim tangentially so both mounting bolts land in the shell wall.
15. Put each25.4 x17.78mm VL53L1X board into its26.1 x18.1mm pod recess. The PCB rear sits at podZ3; a1.6mm PCB has its top at4.6, while the retaining-frame underside is10. Fill the5.4mm edge gap with two required soft closed-cell foam strips18 x2 x6mm per sensor, gently compressed to5.4mm. Place them along the upper and lower PCB edges, centered nearY±8.3; do not place them over the center optical package or the side connectors. Fit the retaining frame with twoM2x14 screws and nuts and verify the board cannot rock. If the purchased PCB thickness differs, measure and adjust the edge shims to provide light preload. Both ±X pod walls have8mm-wide reliefs for the horizontal STEMMA QT plugs. The remaining aperture must leave the emitter and receiver unobscured; the sensor sees through air with no printed optical window. Connect all five sensors before closing the shell.
16. Join the four Adafruit1768 RGB quarter-rings into one60-pixel circle. Dry-fit its158mm OD and145mm ID around the radius76 support posts. Use thin electronics-compatible adhesive strips on the posts to retain the PCB at headZ24. Leave solder-joint and wire clearance at the quadrant joins; check for sharp leads before fitting the diffuser.
17. Set the LCD cradle on the four corner pillars at headZ14, with its USB relief toward the intended cable route. Place the actual LCD in the55.8mm pocket on thin edge foam. The rigid body envelope is17–29; the front glass datum is assumed31.2. Place the corner retaining plate at29–32. FourM3x25 screws go through retainer and cradle into the lower shell pillars. Tighten only until foam is just compressed. Confirm no load is applied to the active glass, and test display/USB operation while it is accessible.
18. Install the white-ring carrier on its separate four cardinal bosses at radius45, headZ32.5, using fourM3x10 screws. It is2mm thick and does not intersect the LCD corner posts or retainer. Retain the Adafruit2862 RGBW ring on it with thin adhesive; the intended PCB underside is34.5, PCB top36.1 and LED top approximately38.1. Check the actual board and LED heights; the optical skin begins at40.8, leaving about2.7mm above the assumed LED top.
19. Place1mm axial shims on each of the four radius60 face bosses and eight radius83 bezel bosses. Fit the outer diffuser with its smooth face outward: its front is42 and its rear flange is35.8–37. The front bezel and center baffle capture the flanges without squeezing the optical skin. The outer flange has screw-clearance notches. The142.6mm center baffle leaves0.2mm radial clearance to the diffuser's143mm inner wall. Secure the center plate with fourM3x12 screws and the outer bezel with eightM3x10 screws.
20. Place the inner translucent diffuser over the RGBW ring and secure it with three tiny removable dots of neutral-cure silicone at its outer edge. Keep its47.8mm opening centered over the active45.68mm screen. Its outer diameter68mm clears the center plate's68.6mm opening. Let adhesive cure fully before operating the rings. The central visible LCD remains recessed; verify the intended viewing angle before making final foam adjustments.

## Optional 4-inch DSI head

The optional head replaces the standard head's shell, centre plate, white ring carrier, inner diffuser, LCD cradle and retainer with `head_shell_dsi`, `lcd4_carrier`, `face_ring_dsi` and `brow_bracket`. It keeps the fork, outer bezel, outer diffuser, the60-pixel RGB halo and all five sensor pods. It has no separate white ring: the halo provides the white lamp function at its capped brightness, so choose this head for a larger animated face rather than for task lighting. The same Z convention applies (rear face0, toward the viewer).

Datums: the display rim front is at35.5 (glass at35.25), the case rear plate at29.5, the M4 boss ends at25.5, the carrier plate at20.5–23.5 with Ø8 pads rising to25.5 under the four bosses, the shell carrier bosses at(±55,±38) topping at20.5, and the face ring at36–40 with its rear face0.5mm in front of the display rim. Components behind the display reach down to17.3 inside the carrier window, above the fork nuts (Z≤9). The face ring's Ø108 rear opening overlaps the display case rim by9mm and its45-degree chamfer widens to Ø116 at the front, leaving about3mm of the case's black rim visible around the Ø101.5 active area. The front ToF pod sits on the brow bracket above the rim, its rear flange at Z32 and its face at44, level with the bezel.

21. Attach `head_shell_dsi` to the fork with fourM3x12 bolts, washers and nuts, exactly as in step14. Fit the rear, left, right and downward pods on their wall mounts with twoM3x10 each. Bolt the brow bracket's base plate to the fourth wall mount at the top of the shell with twoM3x10 and nuts inside; the bracket's cable groove must face outward and open toward its front plate. Fit the front pod to the bracket's front plate with twoM3x10 and nuts, then bring its STEMMA cable through the21 x12 opening, down the groove and through the Ø12 wall hole into the shell. Prepare all five sensors as in step15.
22. Fit the RGB halo on the radius76 posts as in step16. Its inner edge at radius72.4 clears the Ø140 carrier by2.4mm.
23. Screw `lcd4_carrier` to the back of the display before it enters the head: four M4x8 screws through the pads into the case bosses. The carrier window must clear the PCB, the Pi standoffs (leave them installed) and every connector; the notch at the bottom clears the power plug and the panel FPC. Do not use screws longer than8mm. Connect the800mm 15-pin DSI FFC to the connector on the PCB edge inside the carrier window, contacts oriented as the vendor wiki shows, and plug the5V/GND lead into the HP2.0 socket. Fold the FFC back under the carrier.
24. Lower the display and carrier into the shell so the carrier holes land on the(±55,±38) bosses and the four face-ring bosses at radius67.8 pass through the carrier's Ø8.5 clearance notches. Route the FFC and the power lead under the carrier to the26 x8 floor slot. Fix the carrier with fourM3x8 screws from the front; the boss positions lie outside the Ø126 case and stay accessible.
25. Put0.5mm foam tape on the display rim where the face ring will bear, and1mm axial shims on the four radius67.8 bosses and the eight bezel bosses. Fit the outer diffuser as in step19, then `face_ring_dsi` with fourM3x12 screws and the outer bezel with eightM3x10 screws. Tighten the face ring evenly until the foam is just compressed; the ring must not press on the glass inside the Ø101.5 active area.

Connect it to the Pi's DSI connector on the tray and the power lead to header pins4 and39. Configure `/boot/firmware/config.txt` and `display_kind` as the software chapter describes, and check the picture, its rotation and the backlight before closing the base.

## Bring-up and mechanical limits

27. Set each servo ID separately before daisy-chaining its data lines. Index all four neutral counts to2048 with the physical pose above. Start with the head supported, torque disabled, and inspect the complete manually moved limited envelope for cable tension and contact. Then operate the empty structure slowly. Install the head only after the unloaded checks. Initial commanded ranges are yaw±25 degrees and shoulder/elbow/wrist±10 degrees about neutral; these are commissioning limits, not a verified full workspace. The enclosed links are checked digitally only at the indexed pose: confirm by hand that each open clevis clears the previous block, keeper and cables through the commissioned travel before enabling torque.
28. Recheck fasteners and block clamp after the first short motion run and after the first thermal cycle; the servo is enclosed, so read its temperature register during the first sustained runs and open the link if it exceeds the software limit. Read servo load/current/temperature while stationary. Reduce ring brightness during initial enclosed thermal checks. Physical proof-load, drop, fatigue, diffusion, thermal and cable-flex tests remain to be performed on a built sample; mesh checks cannot establish those properties.

## Load and stability calculations

The30kg.cm vendor torque is a stall value, approximately2.94N.m, not a continuous design rating. For preliminary sizing, assume a0.40kg complete standard head including fork, a0.14kg forearm including wrist servo, a0.13kg upper link including elbow servo, and low acceleration; the enclosed halves add roughly10g per link over the revision A rails. With a0.06m effective head offset forward from the wrist, the folded neutral head center is about0.086m ahead of the shoulder. Its gravitational shoulder moment is0.40 x9.81 x0.086=0.34N.m. The forearm's center of mass is nearY-0.017m and the upper link nearY-0.030m, so those masses partially counterbalance the head. Use the absolute sum rather than crediting counterbalance for a conservative neutral estimate:0.34+0.14 x9.81 x0.017+0.13 x9.81 x0.030≈0.40N.m.

The optional 4-inch head is heavier: the display and case are estimated at0.19kg against about0.10kg for the parts they replace, so plan for a0.50kg head and a neutral shoulder moment near0.42+0.06≈0.48N.m. Weigh the built head; if the total exceeds0.50kg, keep the commissioning limits small and repeat the calculation with the measured mass.

If the same assembly is extended horizontally, a0.32m head moment arm alone produces1.26N.m for the standard head (1.57N.m for the optional head), and the links add about0.30N.m. This approaches the stall rating too closely for prolonged operation without a measured thermal/load assessment. The default limited folded pose deliberately avoids claiming that extended posture as continuous-duty capability. Weigh the built head; if it exceeds the assumed mass, reduce reach and motion and repeat the calculations using measured mass.

The1.19kg steel disc alone supplies a nominal restoring moment1.19 x9.81 x0.108≈1.26N.m about the base edge before considering the rest of the base mass. The calculation assumes a flat rigid support and does not include dynamic forces, cable pulls or foot friction. It supports low-acceleration folded commissioning, not a stability certification. Use a commercial padded desk clamp if the measured mass/reach or intended motion requires additional restraint. Do not use the printed lamp as a lifting arm.

## Drawing conventions and verification

Each printable STL has its own local coordinates. The manifest identifies orientation, dimensions, quantities, variant and hardware. Assembly transforms are row-major4x4 matrices with millimetre translations; apply them directly to the STL vertices. The right-hand link halves are placed with a180-degree rotation about the link axis so that the same plate-down print orientation serves both sides. Purchased metal horns, fasteners, wiring and the actual display geometry are not generated by the print exporter. Mechanical projection sheets should be derived from the exact final STLs. `validation/assembly_check.json` records static indexed-neutral printed-part collision results for both assemblies; its scope does not include a proven continuous motion envelope. Keep the final report with the manual.
