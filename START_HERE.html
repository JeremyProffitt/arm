Optional head

The same lamp with a 4-inch face.

Revision B adds an optional head for the Waveshare 4inch DSI LCD (C): a round 720 × 720 display that the Raspberry Pi drives directly and draws the face on itself. It shares the base, the enclosed arm, the fork, the RGB halo and all five sensors with the standard build, and it needs four extra printed parts. See the mechanical chapter of the manual and cad/assembly_dsi.scad.

This is a digital prototype project. The videos are rendered animations of the design, not footage of a physically tested device. The selected Waveshare screens are LCDs, not OLEDs. Print the fit coupon and follow the first-build checks before making a complete unit.
